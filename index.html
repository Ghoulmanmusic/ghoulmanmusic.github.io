02 / Bio

The story behind
the ghoul.

“Prepare for war,
but pray for peace.”

Raised by a single mother of four, Ghoul Man grew up moving between Palestine and Houston. His mother made sure the family had what they needed, but the constant moves made lasting connections difficult. Isolation came early; gaming became an escape.

Music found him there. Hearing Adema’s “Immortal” through Mortal Kombat sparked a love for nu metal and opened the door to other sounds. His first lyrics went into a black notebook his mother gave him in fourth grade. Sharing them would take more time.

After years of being judged for his interests, he learned to embrace his individuality. Today, his music follows that same instinct: moving through darkwave, gothic rock, post-punk, grunge, and cloud rap with an honest voice.

Ghoul Man turns loneliness, faith, betrayal, love, and personal growth into songs that let the vulnerable parts speak.
